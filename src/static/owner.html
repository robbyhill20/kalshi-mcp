Kalshi owner controls

Use this page to check your server and stop trading. Per-order approvals are optional. Keep the owner token out of Grok. Open the hosted /owner URL after deployment; a local HTML file cannot connect.

The emergency stop takes effect before the next submission check. An order already sent may still execute. Clearing it does not enable live trading. Cancel existing orders separately in Kalshi or through Sports Director.

Automatic cash allocation

Kalshi can continually rebalance your prediction-market cash. The target below assigns 40% to exchange 0 (Default), 20% to exchange 1 (Combos), and 40% to exchange 3 (Tennis, Baseball, Basketball). Cash on exchange 2 (Crypto & Commodities) becomes eligible to move when free to do so. Kalshi keeps collateral for resting orders. Displayed balances can differ from the target while orders are open.

This is an owner-only account setting. Grok cannot change it. The setting remains active until you change or disable it through Kalshi.

Optional: approve one exact order

This section is only needed when REQUIRE_TRADE_APPROVAL=true. Each approval expires after 15 minutes and is usable once. Buy price is your maximum cost per contract; sell price is your minimum proceeds per contract.

The unchecked default requires a maker-only order. No price, side or quantity is changed automatically.
